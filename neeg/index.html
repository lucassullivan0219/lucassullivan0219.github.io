NEEG-2 Receiver

Step 0: connection test. Connects straight to the headset's JDY-23 BLE module with Web Bluetooth, subscribes to FFE1 and prints the raw bytes and decoded values.

Devices

    How connecting works / headset not found?
    • No pairing is needed. The phone's own Bluetooth settings cannot connect to the JDY-23; that is expected. Connect from this page.
    • Browsers only let a page use a Bluetooth device after you pick it once in the browser's own device picker ("Add device…"). The picker lists only JDY-style modules (FFE0 service, or a name starting with JDY).
    • Telling headsets apart: once a device sends valid NEEG-2 packets it is tagged NEEG-2 ✓ and kept in this browser. Use ✎ to give it a name such as "Headset 07".
    • With "List every nearby device", most entries are unnamed. Hold the phone next to the headset (strongest signal) or switch the headset off and on and watch which entry disappears and returns.
    • Remembered devices and in-page scanning ("Scan") are experimental browser features. In Chrome, try chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features, set it to Enabled and relaunch. Brave may not offer them at all.
    • Brave turns Web Bluetooth off by default. Enable it at brave://flags (search "Bluetooth"), or use Chrome.
    • The JDY-23 accepts one connection at a time and stops advertising while connected. Unplug the ESP32 dongle and close other BLE apps first.
    • Android: turn on Location, and allow the browser the "Nearby devices" permission (Android 12+) or Location (Android 11 and older).
